RSA's AI Adoption Principles – Aligned with Dual-Path Strategy

Guiding principles for building organizational AI capability at scale through learning-led transformation

Foundation: Learning Agenda + Governance-by-Design + Enterprise Integration
Embedded governance through DPIA compliance, Ethical SteerCo oversight, and regulatory readiness (FCA/GDPR/EU AI Act) | Built on established COE model with cross-functional pods
Business Value + Learning Driven Approach
Align AI initiatives with RSA business objectives (risk-free migration, operational efficiency, broker/customer experience) while prioritizing organizational learning over immediate financial returns.
  • Weight learning value over benefit in early steps (challenge business thinking, expand horizons)
  • Tie to strategic programs (One Commercial, Symphony, Tech Modernization)
  • Deliver measurable outcomes that support 2025 rebrand credibility
  • Build demonstration cases that validate AI-to-value linkage
Source: Toby Harris learning agenda + RSA Strategic Priorities
Agile Delivery Through Cross-Functional Pods
Break down AI delivery into manageable sprints/phases using RSA's established COE model with cross-functional pods empowered with P&L accountability.
  • Leverage existing COE structure (CIO organization as pioneer for tech introduction)
  • Create cross-functional pods (CIO + Business + Data + Compliance)
  • Deliver in demonstrable 8-12 week increments with clear success criteria
  • Partner with business to pilot critical use cases (not CIO working in isolation)
  • Monthly steering cadence with quarterly gate reviews
Source: Data Team meeting + One Commercial principles
Governance by Design (Not Afterthought)
Embed governance, ethics, and compliance from inception through established Ethical SteerCo and Working Group frameworks, with DPIA as regulatory gate.
  • DPIA completion mandatory for all AI initiatives (current gap: only 2 of 48 complete)
  • Route through Ethical Working Group → Ethical SteerCo for approval
  • FCA/GDPR/EU AI Act compliance embedded (not Intact Canada framework)
  • Governance that is "seamless for business to adopt" not burdensome
  • Risk assessment at appropriate level (use case vs. capability)
Source: Andrew Tong governance framework + RSA AI Maturity Assessment
Data Foundation + Enterprise Platform Integration
Connect AI initiatives to Enterprise Data Model (EDM) and strategic platforms (EDP, Power UP, Guidewire) to avoid tactical "Unicorn" solutions and technical debt.
  • Integrate with Intact Enterprise Data Model and EDP roadmap
  • Leverage established platforms: Power UP, Instabase, Radar Live, Guidewire
  • Avoid building throwaway solutions or increasing reliance on systems to be decommissioned
  • Address fragmentation (8 policy admin systems, 2 CRMs) through consolidation
  • Move from tactical data marts to enterprise data architecture
Source: Architecture Blueprint + IT Pain Points Workshop
CI/CD + MLOps for Production Readiness
Implement continuous integration and deployment pipelines with model operations (MLOps) to automate build, testing, deployment, and monitoring at scale.
  • Address current MLOps maturity gaps (model retraining, drift monitoring)
  • Automate validation and testing (move from manual to automated)
  • Build robust Guidewire AI pipeline (currently not mature)
  • Establish model monitoring and performance tracking frameworks
  • Enable rapid iteration while maintaining quality and compliance
Source: IT Pain Points Workshop + Data Team feedback
Comprehensive Documentation + Knowledge Transfer
Deliver system documentation with every AI workstream to enable organizational learning, support scale, and build institutional capability.
  • Business rules, functional specs, solution architecture for each use case
  • Test strategy, test cases, test data, automated test scripts
  • Model documentation (algorithms, training data, performance metrics)
  • Integration patterns and reusable components catalogued
  • Consider AI upskilling and awareness programs for organization
Source: Ethical Working Group TOR + Data Team recommendations
SOURCES & VALIDATION:
Current State Assessment: Based on verified stakeholder interviews (Data Team, Toby Harris CIO, Graham Wilde EA, Andrew Tong Governance), RSA AI Maturity Assessment, Architecture Blueprint v2.0, IT Pain Points Workshop, and RSA Strategic Priorities documentation. Current state includes: 48 AI implementations in Risk Register; established COE model; Ethical SteerCo and Working Group frameworks; program-driven delivery structure (One Commercial, Symphony, Tech Modernization); fragmented systems landscape (8 PAS, 2 CRMs); only 2 of 48 initiatives have completed mandatory DPIA.
Governance Framework: Ethical Use of Data Steering Committee (Chair: UK&I Chief UW Officer SMF23) and Ethical Working Group (Chair: Chief Technical UW Officer) established with Terms of Reference approved. DPIA is mandatory regulatory gate. FCA, GDPR, and EU AI Act compliance requirements differ from Intact Canada framework and must be adapted for UK/EU jurisdiction.
Operating Model Context: RSA uses established COE (Center of Excellence) concept with hub-and-spoke for MI/BI; federated data scientists in business teams (Claims, Pricing); CIO organization pioneers technology then pilots with business partners. Program structure includes: CL Tech & Workflow, CL Pass, Guidewire, Integrated Platform Pricing, Copilot. Cross-functional collaboration essential - "AI is at the top of our list...from overall organization perspective" (Data Team).
Key Pain Points Addressed: Fragmented systems (8 policy admin, 2 CRMs); slow EDP roadmap with tactical data marts; manual ingestion (Instabase not monitoring inbox); immature MLOps (model retraining, drift monitoring); PII governance gaps; siloed organization. One Commercial principle: "Speed is critical...eliminate 'RSA way, NIG way' conversations and move onto 'new way'."
Alignment to Learning Agenda: Principles prioritize Toby Harris framework: "I would weight value in these early steps much more to how much we're learning than how much benefit it's delivering...I'm generally more concerned that we're learning." Graham Wilde requirement: diverse technical learning to build organizational capability across integration patterns.
