Stage 2 & 3: Evaluation Framework + Quadrant Prioritization

RSA AI Value Framework: 10-Lever Scoring System

Every use case evaluated across 10 dimensions (1-5 scale) to determine Business Impact Score and Implementation Effort Score

BUSINESS IMPACT (5 Levers - Equally Weighted at 20%)
  1. Revenue Impact: Direct revenue growth (pricing, new products, policy growth) | 5 = £5M+ annually
  2. Cost Savings: Operational efficiency, automation, fraud reduction | 5 = £5M+ annually
  3. Risk Reduction: UW accuracy, compliance, fraud detection, regulatory | 5 = Critical risk elimination
  4. Broker/Partner Experience: Quote speed, service quality, relationship strength | 5 = Transformative improvement
  5. Strategic Fit: Alignment to One Commercial, ~90% CR target, modernization | 5 = Core to digital transformation
IMPLEMENTATION EFFORT (5 Levers - Equally Weighted at 20%)
  1. Data Readiness: Availability, quality, integration needs | 5 = Data doesn't exist or major infrastructure needed
  2. Technical Complexity: AI sophistication, system integration, novel algorithms | 5 = Cutting-edge agentic AI
  3. Change Impact: Organizational adjustment, process redesign | 5 = Enterprise-wide transformation
  4. Model Risk: Regulatory scrutiny, explainability, customer impact | 5 = High-stakes automated decisions
  5. Adoption Readiness: User eagerness, training needs, cultural barriers | 5 = Major resistance/change management

Scoring Threshold: 3.0 (configurable) | Quadrant Logic: Impact ≥3.0 & Effort <3.0 = Quick Win | Impact ≥3.0 & Effort ≥3.0 = Strategic Bet | Impact <3.0 & Effort <3.0 = Experimental | Impact <3.0 & Effort ≥3.0 = Watchlist

Strategic Quadrant Placement

QUICK WINS
High Impact | Low Effort
Priority: Immediate deployment (40% portfolio allocation)
Example: Email triage, document extraction, broker insights
STRATEGIC BETS
High Impact | High Effort
Priority: Planned investment (30% portfolio allocation)
Example: End-to-end agentic UW, predictive pricing, claims AI pipeline
EXPERIMENTAL
Low Impact | Low Effort
Priority: Learning & innovation (20% portfolio allocation)
Example: ChatGPT search tools, proof-of-concepts, technology pilots
WATCHLIST
Low Impact | High Effort
Priority: Re-evaluate or defer (10% portfolio allocation)
Requires business case refinement or market condition changes
Governance Gate: All use cases must pass through Ethical Working Group → Ethical SteerCo review. DPIA completion is mandatory before moving to delivery. Current gap: Only 2 of 48 initiatives have completed DPIA.

End-to-End Example: Email/Document Triage for Instabase Mailbox

Graham Wilde's Priority Use Case #7: Following a real use case through the complete TOM

Use Case #7: Email/Document Triage for Instabase Mailbox
QUICK WIN
BUSINESS PROBLEM
Brokers email submissions to individual underwriters instead of group mailbox. Manual routing delays triage. Inconsistent ingestion into Instabase AI Hub.
AI SOLUTION
AI model identifies broker submissions in Outlook, classifies intent, routes to Dynamics group mailbox automatically with approval loop initially.
VALUE FRAMEWORK SCORES
Business Impact: 3.6/5 (Revenue: 3, Cost: 4, Risk: 3, Broker: 4, Strategic: 4)
Implementation Effort: 2.2/5 (Data: 2, Technical: 2, Change: 2, Model Risk: 2, Adoption: 3)
→ QUADRANT: Quick Win (High Impact, Low Effort)
T-SHIRT SIZE & INVESTMENT
Size: S | 4-8 weeks | 2-4 people | £140K-£390K investment | £500K-£1.2M projected benefit (3-6x ROI)

5-Stage Journey Through TOM

1. IDEATION
Inside-Out Source:
• Graham Wilde (EA): "Brokers don't use group mailbox, they email Fred directly"
• Pain Point: Manual routing inefficiency
• Strategic Fit: Supports One Commercial UW efficiency goals

Outside-In Validation:
• Intact Canada: Email classification deployed
• Hexaware workshop: Email triage identified as horizontal capability
→
2. EVALUATION
10-Lever Scoring:
• High cost savings (reduce manual routing)
• High broker experience (faster response)
• Low technical complexity (Outlook + Dynamics integration exists)
• Low data needs (email metadata)
• Moderate adoption (UWs need to trust AI routing)

Ethical Review:
• DPIA required (processing email content)
• Approval loop mitigates risk initially
→
3. PRIORITIZATION
Quadrant Placement:
• Quick Win quadrant (Score: 3.6 impact / 2.2 effort)
• Approved for immediate Q1 2025 deployment

Resource Allocation:
• Pod Team: 1 Product Owner (UW lead), 1 Developer (Power Automate), 1 BA, 1 QA
• Budget: ~£265K (mid-point of S sizing)
• Timeline: 6 weeks (2x 2-week sprints + 2 weeks UAT)

Dependencies:
• Dynamics CRM group mailbox setup
• Outlook API access approved
→
4. DELIVERY
Sprint 1-2 (Weeks 1-4):
• Power Automate flow: Outlook → AI classification → Dynamics
• NLP model training on historical broker emails
• Approval UI for underwriters
• Integration with Instabase trigger

Sprint 3 (Weeks 5-6):
• UAT with 5 underwriters
• Feedback loop refinement
• DPIA sign-off from Ethical Working Group

Week 7-8:
• Pilot deployment (10 underwriters)
• Hypercare support
→
5. SCALE
Weeks 9-12:
• Rollout to all Commercial Lines UW team
• Confidence threshold tuning (reduce approval loop)
• Training materials + knowledge transfer

Months 3-6:
• Extend to Specialty Lines
• Horizontal replication to Claims email triage
• Remove approval loop (full automation)

Lessons Learned:
• Document Outlook integration pattern for reuse
• NLP classification model becomes horizontal capability
• Feedback to Value Framework on actual ROI
